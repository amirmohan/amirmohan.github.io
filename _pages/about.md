I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology. I serve as Program Faculty in the Ph.D. programs in [Machine Learning](https://ml.gatech.edu/phd), [Bioinformatics](https://bioinformatics.gatech.edu), and [Bioengineering](https://bioengineering.gatech.edu), and am affiliated with [IDEaS](https://research.gatech.edu/data), [IMS](https://matter-systems.gatech.edu), [SRI](https://space.gatech.edu), and the [Petit Institute for Bioengineering and Bioscience (IBB)](https://research.gatech.edu/bio).

Before joining Georgia Tech, I was a postdoctoral researcher at [Stanford](https://www.stanford.edu) and [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu), and I earned my Ph.D. from [Rice University](https://www.rice.edu/). 

My research develops the **algorithmic foundations of ML/AI for biology**. My group traces how biological foundation models represent information and use it to make predictions, develops methods to control their behavior, and designs faster sampling algorithms for discrete generative models. We also develop AI-enabled inverse methods that recover more information from fewer molecular measurements across microscopy, mutational scanning, and mass spectrometry.

Current research directions include:

- Mechanistic interpretability and steering of protein foundation models
- Efficient and controllable discrete generative models
- Scientific agents, autonomous discovery, and AI biosecurity
- Computational sensing and inverse problems for cryo-EM and electron ptychography
